Our Story

A Cozy Escape in the Heart of Burtibang

Nestled along the serene banks near Laxmikunda Pul, Bamboo Garden Restaurant & Bar is Burtibang's premier dining destination. Our bamboo-themed interiors and warm ambient lighting create the perfect setting for memorable meals.

From traditional Nepali thalis to signature cocktails, every dish is crafted with local ingredients and served with heartfelt hospitality.
